## Qualification sequence after authorization

1. Verify the exact source and environment against the owner decision before opening a database connection. Recheck the supported migration history, physical target, ownership, grants and enforced RLS as required by the approved installer. Stop on drift or uncertainty. Preserve the frozen migrations and historical R6 evidence.
2. Exercise the approved installer and exact roles on the nominated acceptance target. Test duplicate delivery, write fencing, commit-time authority and credential expiry, interrupted COMMIT acknowledgement, audited quarantine recovery, and real restore/recovery with the approved operational roles. Keep pricing and publication authority in the backend.
3. Verify snapshot → immutable draft → independent approval → activation → outbox → authorized payment and kiosk readback using an approved qualification revision. Verify rejected and cross-tenant commands have no effect. Test business rollback by publishing a fresh immutable revision and retaining its history and committed audit. Use the owner-approved restoration procedure for database recovery.
4. Record SQL and manifest digests, granted roles, scoped evidence and the applicable release decision. The disposable installer and worker evidence establishes the local baseline. Operational composition and the controlled R6 launcher still need their approved target and authorization. [R6 admission and execution requirements](../runtime-qualification.md) · [Channel qualification](kiosk-access.md)

Capture tenant/RLS and grant checks, fresh authority expiry, attempts and transactional outbox fencing, lock contention, cancellation, uncertain commit and cleanup. Include source and executable digests, elapsed times and sanitized evidence references. Block on stale pins, identity mismatch, ownership ambiguity or unverified cleanup. See [R6 qualification procedure](../runtime-qualification.md).

Record the approved manifest digest, target and scope, reviewer, authorization reference, observed results, deviations, sanitized evidence references, teardown and acceptance decision in the owner execution record. A separate scoped decision is required for execution.
